# Privacy Policy – Easy Language

This page explains in simple English what personal data may be used.

**Important:** This is only a simple explanation. It does not replace the [full Privacy Policy](../PRIVACY-POLICY.md) and is not legal advice.

## 1. Who is responsible?
Schnecke-Schneck Creator Studio is responsible for the data processing described in the full Privacy Policy. Full contact details are in the [Privacy Policy](../PRIVACY-POLICY.md) and [Imprint](../IMPRINT.md).

## 2. Website visits
Technical information may be sent to the server. This may include your IP address, date and time of your visit, pages opened, browser and operating system. This helps to run and protect the website.

## 3. Schneckenshop orders
The shop works with Spreadshirt. Information needed for an order may be sent to Spreadshirt. This may include your name, delivery address, email address and payment information. Spreadshirt handles the order, makes the product and ships it. Its privacy rules also apply.

## 4. WhatsApp and email
When you contact the Creator Studio, data needed to answer you may be processed. WhatsApp data may include your phone number, profile name and picture, messages, media and technical details. An AI agent may answer WhatsApp messages. Human support may take over if requested. Emails are handled by a person without AI.

Data about spam, unwanted calls, code requests or bot attacks may be stored to protect systems and block senders.

## 5. Advertising and cookies
The website may show adverts from providers such as Adsterra. Cookies or similar tools may be used. Cookies are small pieces of information that a website can store on your device. You can change or withdraw your choice in the cookie settings. Ad blockers may stop parts of the website from working correctly.

## 6. Reviews and sharing data
The operator may report insulting content or false statements. Personal data is generally not shared. Exceptions may include your permission, an order handled by Spreadshirt, protecting rights, preventing spam or a legal duty.

## 7. Your rights
You have rights under the GDPR. You can ask what data is stored about you. In some cases, you can ask for data to be corrected or deleted, or object to its use.

## 8. Questions
For questions, use the contact details in the full Privacy Policy.
