# Terms and Community Guidelines – Easy Language

This page explains the rules of Schnecke-Schneck Creator Studio in simple English.

**Important:** This is a short explanation. It does not replace the [full terms](../TERMS-AND-COMMUNITY-GUIDELINES.md).

## 1. Protecting content
Texts, pictures, videos, designs and products made by Schnecke Schneck are protected. Do not copy, change, record or present them as your own without permission. Unauthorised use may be reported.

## 2. Contact and spam
The email address and WhatsApp number are for written messages. **Please do not call.** Spam, unwanted adverts, automatic bot messages and requests for login or verification codes are not allowed. A person who misuses the contact details may be blocked and reported.

## 3. Respect
Treat everyone with respect. Insults and discrimination are not allowed. Do not attack people because of their appearance, language, origin or other personal details. These rules also apply to chats and TikTok livestreams. People who break the rules may be blocked and reported. Schnecke Schneck decides what content to publish.

## 4. Schneckenshop
Products are made after an order is placed. Spreadshirt prints and ships them. Spreadshirt's rules also apply to payment, delivery, returns and cancellation rights.

## 5. Fake profiles
Pictures and profile photos may be checked with tools such as Google Lens. If an account is found to be fake, the chat may be ended and the account may be blocked or reported. Read the full terms for details about chat data.

## 6. Honest contact
Be honest when contacting others or using dating features. Fake profiles, lies, insults and false information are not accepted. Accounts may be deleted, blocked or reported.

## 7. Social media and support
The rules of each social media service also apply, including TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and Telegram. An AI agent may answer WhatsApp messages. You can ask for human support if needed. Emails are answered by a person without AI. Replies may take some time.
